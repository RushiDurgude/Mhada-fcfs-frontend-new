Income : Family income comprises the combined income of the applicant and their spouse derived from any occupation, trade, business, employment, or other source constituting normal means of livelihood. As proof of income, the applicant may submit either an Income Tax Return (ITR) or an income certificate issued by the Tahsildar's office (Maharashtra state).If the applicant is married and both are working, then they must submit their individual income proofs (Separate ITR of self and spouse or Income certificate having combined income of self and spouse). For state government employees, central government employees, and MHADA employees, an ITR is mandatory.
