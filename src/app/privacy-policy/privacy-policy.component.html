Privacy Policy
Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority known as a ‘CIDCO’ is a government organization that provides affordable houses for citizen who do not owns any house. This privacy policy sets out how CIDCO, uses and protects any information that you give us when you use this website/mobile application (individually and collectively, as Platform).
The CIDCO is committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected. Should we ask you to provide any information by which you can be identified when using this Platform, be rest assured that it will only be used strictly in accordance with this Policy.
Please note that our Policy is subject to change at any time, and we may not be able to provide you prior notice. To ensure you are up to date with the Policy, please review this page periodically. This Policy applies to current and former visitors and customers of our Platform. By visiting and/or using our Platform, you are accepting and consenting to the practices described in this Policy.
INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE US
We receive and store the information you explicitly provide to us. This includes personal information such as your username along with your password, your name, telephone number, e-mail address, Address, Aadhar, PAN, Income, Caste, Domicile and other contact information and any other information that you provide during your time on the Platform. You may choose not to provide certain information, but this could prevent you from taking full advantage of our Platforms various features.
What personal information do we collect from the people that visit our blog, website or app?
When registering on our site, as appropriate, you may be asked to enter / upload your PAN number, Name, eMail Address, Phone Number & few more documents like Aadhar Card, Income Certificate, Caste Certificate, Domicile Certificate etc. to help you with your experience.
When do we collect information?
We collect this information from you when you register on our site, fill out a form or enter information on our website / mobile app.
How do we use your information?
We collect personal data such as Name, Address, Aadhar, PAN, Income, Caste, Domicile etc. for deciding the eligibility of the user and to provide the user with Affordable Houses as per the eligibility criteria in the State of Maharashtra under the various schemes of Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority (CIDCO)
We use the information we collect from you when you register, sign up for affordable housing, or use certain other site features in the following ways:
  • To allow us to better service you in responding to your customer feedback / issues.
  • To send periodic emails regarding your application / issues status or other services.
How do we protect visitor information?
  • We do not use vulnerability scanning and/or scanning to PCI standards.
  • We do use an SSL certificate.
  • We have firewall & other security features to protect the data collected and is in custody of Government of Maharashtra.
